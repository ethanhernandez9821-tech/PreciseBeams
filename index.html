Safe Discord Webhook Setup

Follow these steps carefully: On the left side of your screen on Discord, click + button "Add a Server" and click Create my Own. Your server is created and you have the #general channel which is all you need, now click on the settings of your general channel. Click on "Integrations" and then on "Webhooks" and just create a new webhook and copy it. Go to ⁠:link:・sites channel to find the website links, and log in with your discord account to access the website. You are now on the home page. On the left side, click on "settings" and there you will find Webhooks for Default, Robux, Summary and RAP. Paste your copied webhook in all of these 4 and save changes. Your are now done with the basics and you set up everything to start beaming. When you go down a bit, you will find Profile, Group and VIP Server. Those are the available phishing links you can use. Whichever link you want to use, do not forget to hyperlink it.
